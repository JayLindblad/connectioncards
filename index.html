01 — The card

An analog solution
to a digital problem.

We spend so much time connecting online that we've kind of forgotten how to do it in person. You see someone interesting at a bar, on the train, at a show, and you just... don't say anything.

The connection card is a way around that. It's small, it's charming, and it does the awkward part for you. No apps. No algorithms. Just a little piece of paper and a bit of bravery.

Taking inspiration from 19th century "calling cards" (how people showed their intent to socialize) updated for someone who just really liked the vibe.

02 — Why bother
I.
It's more fun

Meeting people in real life with a physical thing in your hand is just better than a follow request. More memorable too.

II.
It takes guts

Handing someone a connection card requires a moment of actual bravery (and a little insanity). Luckily the people worth knowing tend to appreciate that.

III.
It pays forward

Hopefully I gave you more than one card. The idea is that you pass them along too. A little chain of strangers becoming less strange.

04 — Pass it on

You probably got
an extra card or two.

That's intentional. The idea is simple: if you know someone who'd appreciate a card — hand it to them. No explanation needed. That's the whole game.


Think of it as a little chain of strangers becoming slightly less strange. One card at a time.

For a brave friend →
Play cupid. If you have someone in mind and you spot the perfect match, send them on their way.
For a lucky duck →
Show someone you care. It's a small gesture, but it can go a long way.
For someone else →
Got nothing? Pass it on to someone who might.
