01 — The card

An analog solution
to a digital problem.

We spend so much time connecting online that we've kind of forgotten how to do it in person. You see someone interesting at a bar, on the train, at a show and you just... don't say anything.

The acquaintance card is a way around that. It's small, it's charming, and it does the awkward part for you. No apps. No algorithms. Just a little piece of paper and a bit of bravery.

It's inspired by Victorian calling cards (the original way people introduced themselves) updated for someone who just really liked the vibe.

02 — Why bother
I.
It's more fun

Meeting people in real life with a physical thing in your hand is just better than a follow request. More memorable too.

II.
It takes guts

Handing someone a card requires a moment of actual bravery (and a little insanity). That's rare. And the people worth knowing usually appreciate it.

III.
It pays forward

Each card comes with extras. The idea is that you pass them along too. A little chain of strangers becoming less strange.
